1. Course Overview & Objectives

Theory of Computation introduces the foundational mathematical models of computation, formal languages, grammars, and abstract computing machines. Students explore the capabilities and fundamental limitations of computers by investigating finite state machines, context-free grammars, pushdown automata, and Turing machines, concluding with the boundaries of decidability.

Prerequisites

Calculus, Discrete Mathematics, and Data Structures & Algorithms.

NEP 2020 & Experiential Alignment

Blended pedagogy integrating mathematical problem solving with AI-assisted automata simulators, interactive state machines, and real-world compiler tokenizer design cases.
